# UK Labour Market Briefing for Workforce Planning

Vacancies, unemployment, employment, inactivity and regular pay growth from 2019 to June 2026, and what the change means for hiring, pay and retention plans.

- **Data:** six ONS Labour Market Statistics series (AP2Y, MGSC, MGSX, LF24, LF2S, KAI9), release of 15 September 2026, in `data/`. Source: Office for National Statistics, licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
- **Python:** `python/labour_market.py`
- **Outputs:** joined monthly table, `summary.json` and three charts in `outputs/`
- **Report:** https://olajumokemedunoye.github.io/projects/labour-market.html

Run: `python python/labour_market.py` from this folder.
