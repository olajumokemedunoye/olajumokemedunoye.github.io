01
Portfolio project

Workforce Planning & Headcount Forecasting

A scenario model that lets leaders test a hiring plan before committing budget.

  • SQL
  • Excel
  • Power BI
  • Forecasting
  • Scenario modelling
View case studyHide case study

Business problem

Each department planned its own headcount as needs came up. Leadership had no way to stress-test a resourcing decision before committing budget.

Data

Current headcount, historical attrition, hiring lead times and department growth targets.

Analysis

  • Extracted headcount and historical attrition with SQL.
  • Built growth, steady-state and reduction scenarios in Excel, each driven by adjustable assumptions for attrition, lead time and growth.
  • Published the scenarios in Power BI so leaders could switch between cases and see the resourcing gap by department.

Tools

SQL, Excel, Power BI.

Key findings

Two departments carried a structural understaffing risk that standard headcount reports did not show.

Business implication

For those departments, waiting for gaps to appear before hiring would leave them short for the whole hiring lead time.

Recommendation

Plan hiring for the two flagged departments ahead of demand, and review the model's assumptions every quarter.

Outcome

A scenario model leaders can use to test a hiring plan against lead times and attrition before committing budget.

02
Portfolio project

HR Attrition & Cost-of-Turnover Analysis

Put a cost on attrition so that retention could be argued as a business case.

  • SQL
  • Excel
  • Power BI
  • Data cleaning
  • Segmentation
View case studyHide case study

Business problem

Leadership believed attrition was high but had no costed figure to justify spending on retention. Headcount reports showed who left, not what leaving cost.

Data

Departure records, salary data, recruitment spend and onboarding cost estimates.

Analysis

  • Pulled departure and salary records with SQL.
  • Built a cost-per-departure formula in Excel covering recruitment, onboarding and productivity ramp-up.
  • Applied it to every departure in the period, then segmented the total by tenure band, department and performance band in Power BI.
  • Checked the total against payroll figures before presenting it.

Tools

SQL, Excel, Power BI.

Key findings

£312K a year in turnover cost that headcount reporting didn't capture (portfolio project figure). A few departments and the early-tenure leavers carried a disproportionate share of it.

Business implication

With a cost attached, retention became a business case leadership could fund.

Recommendation

Focus retention spend on the departments and tenure bands carrying the most cost, starting with early-tenure leavers.

Outcome

A costed, department-level view that let leadership set retention priorities.

03
Portfolio project

Recruitment Pipeline & Hiring Analytics

Showed where candidates drop out of the hiring process and how long each stage takes.

  • Excel
  • Power BI
  • Data cleaning
  • Funnel analysis
View case studyHide case study

Business problem

Recruiters and hiring managers couldn't see where candidates were dropping out, or why time-to-hire was rising.

Data

An applicant tracking system (ATS) export showing each candidate's movement through the stages, with timestamps and source channel.

Analysis

  • Restructured the export in Excel into a funnel: applied, screened, interviewed, offered, hired.
  • Calculated the conversion rate and the time spent at each stage.
  • Built a Power BI dashboard breaking the funnel down by role, source channel and recruiter.
  • Modelled what would happen if more roles were filled through internal recruitment.

Tools

Excel, Power BI.

Key findings

Drop-off was concentrated among candidates from external channels, not spread evenly through the process.

Business implication

The time-to-hire problem sat in specific channels and stages, so fixes could be targeted instead of changing the whole process.

Recommendation

Fill more suitable roles internally, and track time at each stage on the dashboard.

Outcome

A funnel view of conversion and time-to-hire by role, channel and recruiter that recruiters can use to find bottlenecks.

04
Portfolio project

HR Reporting Automation

Replaced separate departmental spreadsheets with one refreshable performance and productivity report.

  • Excel
  • Power BI
  • Data modelling
  • Data cleaning
View case studyHide case study

Business problem

Performance and productivity data sat in each department's own spreadsheet. There was no single view for spotting underperformance early or tracking change across review cycles.

Data

Quarterly performance ratings and team productivity metrics from the departmental spreadsheets.

Analysis

  • Consolidated the spreadsheets into one structured data model.
  • Standardised the rating scales and productivity measures so departments could be compared directly.
  • Built a Power BI report with filters for team, performance band and period.
  • Set the report to refresh each quarter to match the review cycle.

Tools

Excel, Power BI.

Key findings

Comparing departments only worked once they shared one rating scale. The separate spreadsheets had hidden the differences.

Business implication

Quarterly reporting became a refresh instead of a manual assembly job, and managers saw the same numbers.

Recommendation

Keep a single agreed rating scale across departments and refresh the report on the review cycle.

Outcome

One report where managers can filter to underperforming teams and track movement from quarter to quarter.

Portfolio project

Workforce Attrition Risk Modelling

A classification model that gives each employee an attrition risk score, reported by department.

  • Python
  • SQL
  • Power BI
View projectHide project

Problem

HR only learned someone was at risk once they had resigned, so retention conversations came too late.

Data

Tenure, engagement survey scores, performance ratings, manager-change history and absence patterns.

Analysis

  • Joined the workforce records into one table per employee with SQL.
  • Engineered features in Python (pandas): tenure bands, engagement trend and manager-change flags.
  • Trained a classification model (scikit-learn) and checked its output against known historical leavers.
  • Reported risk by department in Power BI.

Limitations and responsible use

  • Evaluation results (precision, recall, ROC-AUC, confusion matrix) will be published with the code. Until then, read this as a description of the method.
  • A risk score should prompt a conversation, not decide anything. It shouldn't feed into performance, pay or disciplinary decisions.
  • Employee-level scores need restricted access, a documented lawful basis and checks for bias across protected characteristics before any live use.
